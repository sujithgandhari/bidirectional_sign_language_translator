Sign-to-Text Recognition

👋 Sign → Text

Real-time recognition of ASL/ISL gestures from your camera, converted into grammatically correct text for hearing users.

📋 Show Sign → Text Steps ▼
1
Camera Capture
Mobile camera records the signer's hand and upper-body in real time at 30–60 FPS.
2
Preprocessing & ROI
Frames are resized, normalised, and Region of Interest (hands/face) is extracted via MediaPipe.
3
Hybrid CNN + RFC Recognition
CNN extracts deep features; Random Forest Classifier predicts letters, gloss tokens, or words.
4
Gloss / Intermediate Text
Predicted units buffer into sequences, forming raw phrases reflecting sign order and timing.
5
LLM Refinement
Transformer LLM cleans grammar, resolves context and converts gloss into fluent natural language.
6
Text / Speech Output
Final message is displayed as text or spoken aloud via TTS for the hearing user.
Text-to-Sign Synthesis

💬 Text → Sign

Natural language from hearing users is transformed into smooth ISL/ASL sign videos for deaf or hard-of-hearing users.

📋 Show Text → Sign Steps ▼
1
Text / Speech Input
The hearing user types or speaks; ASR converts speech into raw text.
2
NLP + Gloss Generation
The language module simplifies the sentence and generates gloss tailored for the target sign language.
3
Gesture Planning
Each gloss token maps to ASL/ISL gestures, creating a sequence of key poses and transitions.
4
RIFE-Net Interpolation
RIFE-Net generates intermediate frames between key poses, producing smooth 60 FPS sign motion without jitter.
5
Video Rendering
Synthesised sign video is rendered in the app's player and can be replayed or slowed down.
6
Deaf User View
The deaf user watches the final sign animation, completing the text-to-sign communication loop.
